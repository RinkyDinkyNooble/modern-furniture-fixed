# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-03)

First version, for Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47, made from MDM 26.9. It replaces MDM and keeps its mod id and block ids.

- Hitboxes follow the model for every block, including tilted parts.
- 91 pieces of furniture larger than one block are split into one block per space they fill. Placing places every part; breaking any part breaks the whole piece (server setting `breakWholePiece`).
- 108 pieces have storage that opens as a vanilla chest, with a size per block (`storageRows`), a short title, and barrel sounds that each player can turn off (`furnitureSounds`). Beds open from their long sides.
- Every block mines faster with an axe or a pickaxe.
- Repaired: the pouf model, the office desk ends' cabinets, the left office desk variant 3's model, the wenge wardrobe's colour, the wenge nightstand's facing, the end of bathroom shelf 2, and the x-ray next to set 1 corner cabinet 2.
- Removed: the boots, shoe racks and electric guitars. Storage removed from the white loveseat, the kitchen islands, the set 2 corner counter and the set 2 hanging shelf.
